About My Department

Introduction

The Software Engineering Department is one of the most active departments in the university. It focuses on training students in programming, networking, and system design, preparing them for careers in the technology industry.

History of the Department

The department was established to meet the growing demand for skilled computing professionals. Over the years, it has grown from a small unit into one of the largest departments, with modern laboratories and a wide range of courses.

Courses Offered

Students in the department study courses such as programming languages, data structures, databases, and web deveopment. Practical sessions are help regularly to reinforce classroom learning.

Important Notice

Note: All students must register for practical sessions before the end of the semester.
